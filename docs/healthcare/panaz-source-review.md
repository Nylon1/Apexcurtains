# Panaz source review

Reviewed 2026-11-11. This is an evidence register, not a claim that the full Panaz healthcare range has been imported.

## Publication and rights

The Apex owner confirmed existing written permission to republish Panaz product images during this implementation. Rights basis: **owner attestation, 2026-11-11**. The agreement itself and its storage location were not supplied. Retain the agreement in Apex's supplier records and check its scope before future uses.

Nine original embedded photographs were extracted without retouching from the accessible official [September 2025 curtain basecloth card](https://panaz.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Curtain-Basecloth-card-Sept.2025-WEB.pdf). Page 1 was visually inspected to match labels to photographs. These are **basecloth texture photographs**, not printed design colourways or installation photographs. Most are only about 244 px wide: suitable for modest thumbnails, not detailed enlarged weave claims. Request high-resolution originals for the full gallery. Mapping and rights provenance are in `panaz-image-provenance.json`.

## Further eligible research candidates

The September 2025 card also marks Diamond Weave 459/083, Plainweave Light 222, Twill Pinstripe 288, Satin Filament 436 and Duo Blackout 447 for cubicle use. Cambric Light 317/437 is sheer; do not imply visual privacy. Confirm healthcare suitability for the specific facility before promotion. Matching basecloth images are supplied where accessible; these candidates are not a verified printed-colourway catalogue.

The official [collection page](https://panaz.com/collection/base-cloth/) identifies Micro Blackout 235 and Textured Blackout 187 for window curtains. It identifies Highland Print Textured 010, Panvelle Luxe Waterproof 340, Panvelle Waterproof 004 and Woolton 285 as upholstery; exclude those from curtain imports. Its Textured Blackout 187 entry shows a conflicting colourway label of “235 Micro Blackout”; do not copy that mismatched colourway or asset mapping.

## Printed designs and colourways

The official [healthcare digital-print directory](https://panaz.com/healthcare/digital-print/) lists named designs including Arbor, Arley, Aura, Balance, Balham, Breeze, Cascade, Celeste, Dune, Eden, Grasslands, Hare, Orchard, Seagrass and Seascape. This is a discovery list, not proof of any particular design/basecloth/colourway combination.

The [Textures collection](https://panaz.com/collection/textures/) has current curtain/healthcare entries, including Malin 192 Ink, 196 Heritage Blue, 321 Wheat, 401 Amber, 409 Magenta, 412 Purple, 642 Thistle, 700 Brown, 806 Camel and 807 Mushroom. Material, flammability and colour-fastness are explicitly basecloth-dependent. Do not create a Cartesian product of these names and the five cubicle bases. Supplier-approved combinations and correctly matched high-resolution images remain required.

## Outstanding supplier inputs

- Authorised current trade export with design, colourway, exact basecloth, availability and repeat dimensions.
- Current application-specific technical sheets and fire certificates for each combination.
- Full care protocols, including thermal disinfection limits and ShieldPlus durability after laundering.
- High-resolution licensed swatches, texture images and installation images with precise asset-to-product identifiers.
- Permission agreement reference, restrictions, attribution requirements and any expiry.
- Trade prices, lead times, printed-on-both-sides references and ordering limits. Do not use older brochure minimum quantities as current commercial promises.

No printed design colourway has been validated sufficiently for publication by this review. Five priority basecloth records have identity, application and basic technical evidence; nine genuine basecloth photographs have been supplied under owner-attested rights.
