## Forest MTS

[Official UK MTS page](https://www.forestgroup.co.uk/uk/products/mts-medical-track-system): healthcare partition tracks, manual operation, white powder-coated aluminium. Dimensions: Standard **19.6 × 30mm**, Flat **15 × 33mm**, Round **28 × 28mm**. The page lists rail mass **500g/m**; this is not curtain load capacity. Only **MTS Standard** has the confirmed BioCote treatment. The overview says “Straight” while the detailed heading says “Flat”; use **MTS Flat**, noting Forest also uses Straight in the overview. Straight runs and bent layouts are supported. Ceiling/fixing design needs project assessment.

[2026 MTS brochure](https://www.forestgroup.com/user_files/download_files/download_2090_FOREST_MTS_EN_120126_WEB.pdf), document BR0010EN080126, provides the more precise ordering table:

The brochure confirms wall, suspended-ceiling and V-shaped suspension brackets; short metal-hook carrier **4510101000** supports all three variants. Standard wall bracket **4520001000**, Round **4516051000**, Flat **4576051000**. Corresponding end caps: **4516101000**, **4516151000**, **4576151000**. Suspension bracket **4518201000** covers 10–100cm; **4518201500** covers 100–150cm. Inside/reverse bends use a **20cm radius**; continuous curves are factory special order. The brochure's curve drawing and web page disagree about continuous-curve minimum dimensions, so obtain a drawing-specific confirmation rather than publishing the web page's 50cm-diameter claim. Do not infer Flat availability at 5.8m from the general page. Structural capacity, bracket spacing, substrate fixing, cleaning chemicals and exact warranty conditions remain to be confirmed. Brochure marketing mentions ten years, but project coverage was not established.

**Compatibility status:** Forest's own metal-hook carrier is documented. Apex curtain headings must be specified to that carrier and approved dimensions. Behrens fixed hooks/EasyClick are not documented as fitting Forest. Universal eyelets are a potential route requiring a technical check of the actual carrier/hook, curtain load and geometry. Do not mark any Forest/Behrens combination verified from these sources.

## Bespoke, certification and replacement dependencies

[Bespoke sizing source](https://divisions.behrens.co.uk/product/bespoke-size-disposable-curtains/) advertises up to **754cm width × 280cm drop**, supplier cutting of stocked material in one week, and unique production in 8–10 weeks. These are public-source indications, not directly reconfirmed 2026 lead-time commitments. No MOQ is stated. The text concerns **Behrens** production/cutting: it does not authorise Apex trimming, or establish the effect of alteration on certification. Keep Apex trimming unavailable pending explicit supplier permission and written technical confirmation.

[Official FR certificate](https://divisions.behrens.co.uk/media/FR-Disposable-Certificate.pdf) is IFS report **23975**, issued **12 August 2013**, job **FPC10599**, for Francis Price Contracts, described as FR polypropylene hospital window/cubicle curtains. Colour is unstated. The tested sample passed **BS 5867:2008 Part 2 Type C**, before/after the stated watersoak procedure. The document expressly limits results to the tested item and says it shall not be duplicated. Link to the supplier-hosted document; do not mirror it or treat it as certification of every current colour, printed design, hook configuration or altered curtain. Obtain current SKU-linked evidence and permitted handling instructions before publishing a definitive standard against individual products.

For replacement services, retain the customer-approved track model, hook, dimensions, room identifier, quantities and revision date in the approved secure project system. Repeat orders still need product availability and compatibility checks. Replacement timing is determined by the facility's infection-control policy; this review establishes no universal interval.

## Release gates

- Forest: publish only exact documented profiles/lengths; technical check for structure, loading, fixing intervals, cleaning, continuous curves and every non-Forest curtain interface.
- Behrens: image delivery is blocked by access restrictions despite owner-attested permission; supplier media files are needed. Current FR/SKU applicability, hook-model fit and care must remain explicit unknowns.
- All suppliers: record written licence location/conditions, supplier trade account/supply route, availability, and project-specific configuration approval. None of those were independently confirmed in this research.
