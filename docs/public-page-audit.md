- Reworked the 71 local service pages and the 36-region directory. City-specific text, established URLs, FAQ data and local organisation identities remain. Oxford, Cambridge and Peterborough link an actual local case. Other towns link the wider portfolio.
- Replaced competing Contact actions with direct phone and email, a guided quote for individual windows and a planner for architectural or trade briefs. The original quotation options, advice, projects and service-area links remain accessible.
- Reworked Advice as a reading index. All ten current CMS articles appear once across the two article lists. Three reviewed articles have specific headings, excerpts and metadata. Existing CMS paragraphs are now semantic text paragraphs. Article image metadata accepts absolute and relative sources.
- The reviewed copy is version-controlled. Its public projection recognises a legacy passage in each affected record and also applies to related-article and index entries. The CMS records were not edited. When publishing replacements in the CMS, update the title, excerpt, body and metadata together and remove the corresponding legacy passage.
- Added workroom and high-level installation decisions to Services. Added section links to common questions and technical references where present in the architectural guides.
- Connected the 14 portfolio records to the architectural planner alongside the original quote. Removed the redundant summary fallback, raised fact-label contrast and brought the photograph ahead of a long introduction on phones.

## Development priorities after review

1. **Capture one tall rectangular installation properly.** Ask for matching open and closed photographs, an optional control or track close-up, and a project record that separates glazing height, hanging line and finished drop. Place the approved case on the homepage and tall or motorised guide where it supports the text.
2. **Measure whether customers choose the right enquiry.** For an initial four-week baseline, compare contact-to-guided-quote and contact-to-planner starts, completion by entry route, and the proportion the sales team considers appropriately routed. Keep names, photographs and other enquiry contents out of analytics. Use the baseline to decide whether either route needs simplifying.
3. **Resolve the measuring-article overlap.** When Search Console access is available, compare landing URLs and queries for the two measuring articles, then review their enquiries with the team. Keep distinct intents if the data supports them. Review redirects and inbound links before any consolidation.
4. **Improve technical proof at the point of decision.** Start with a documented fabric join and an open-stack photograph. Check them with the workroom or installer and publish their verified context alongside the explanatory diagram.
5. **Run a procurement usability session.** Ask a facilities buyer to compare two named cloth references, choose a compatible track and prepare an enquiry on a phone. Record where an abbreviation, sample or document is missing before expanding the existing catalogue.
