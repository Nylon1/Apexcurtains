## Final visual findings

### Apex curtains

Jesmond’s full elevation and separate sheer line lead the page. The larger diagram labels clarify shaped support, retained cloth and the horizontal inner layer.

### Gable-end curtains

Windermere’s open/closed comparison creates a distinct paired opening. Both photographs preserve the gable, with matching captions and clear links to the original story.

### Tall & double-height curtains

A portrait dining-room image establishes the vertical scale. The fitting film and height diagram connect the room’s proportions to the practical decisions.

### Oversized architectural curtains

The wide airport-lounge opening and three-column design section suit expansive interiors. Images retain the curtain run and surrounding architecture.

### Motorised curtain systems

The deep-green film opening demonstrates real operation. Native controls, a separate voile film and the existing working simulation make the page distinct.

### High-level curtain installation

The reversed desktop opening leads with genuine fitting activity. A three-stage process, fitting film and access diagram explain the service without presenting the photographed equipment as a universal method.

### Specialist curtain manufacturing

Norwich’s cloth detail, warm background and seam diagram establish a material-focused identity. Installed details are labelled honestly, with a clear manufacture-only/trade pathway.

## Refinements made in this pass

- Increased diagram label sizes, scoped to these seven pages; all labels stay within their SVG view boxes.
- Tightened mobile opening typography and spacing so photography appears earlier, while retaining prominent consultation buttons.
- Simplified the customer-review link label. No new quotations, specifications or unrelated products were added.
- Kept full photographic proportions, native film controls and the cream, green and bronze Apex palette. Page openings vary through paired images, portrait scale, broad architecture, film, fitting activity and cloth detail.
