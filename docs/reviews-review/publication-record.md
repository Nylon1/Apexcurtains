## Before publishing a customer account

Record separately:

1. Original customer text, its platform permalink or protected evidence reference, source date where recorded and the date checked.
2. Approved excerpt and display name, the documented publication basis and scope, and approval owner/date. Privately supplied feedback needs clear permission; published-source use must have an appropriate documented basis.
3. Optional portfolio project slug, evidence connecting that account to the installation, and separate photograph publication permission. A genuine review does not require a project association.

Do not infer review dates from website edits, star ratings from praise, identities from display labels, or associations from matching towns. Keep private correspondence and contact evidence out of public data. Use a single verified record referenced by existing pages; do not duplicate portfolio records.

The exact Apex Google Business Profile URL remains unconfirmed. Do not publish guessed platform links, Google attribution, ratings or counts. Similar-name businesses found in research were excluded. Self-serving LocalBusiness/Organization review markup must not be used to promise Google review-star results.
