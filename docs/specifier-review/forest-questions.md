# Forest technical clarification questions

APX-FQ01 · Revision R01 · 27 November 2026 · For supplier response — not sent

Purpose: obtain traceable project-selection guidance for Forest curtain systems. P01 withholds numerical product specifications while clarification and approval remain outstanding. Please identify the responding technical representative, date, applicable product codes, regional variants and current drawing/manual revisions.

1. **Motor envelope:** For each proposed Shuttle variant, which current drawing defines the body, complete fitted assembly, attachment movement and removal envelope? Please reconcile the different datums used in the Shuttle Series brochure and S/M/L installation manual, and identify any obsolete versions.
2. **Recess and access:** For the selected FMS/FMS Plus or recess generation, what drawings govern the drive end, pulley, motor orientation, connectors, cable bending and maintenance access? Which details change with heading, curvature or installation sequence?
3. **System selection:** What information do you need to confirm suitability for actual curtain weight, lining, heading, track route, bends, joints, draw direction and duty? Please distinguish product maxima from approval of the complete proposed configuration.
4. **Electrical and controls:** For the exact regional motor/control codes, confirm supply and protection requirements, isolation, connection method, power continuity, separate power-supply access and required interface equipment. Which tasks and commissioning checks require the electrical contractor or controls integrator?
5. **Support and mounting:** Which current bracket, mounting and track-interface documents apply? Identify the information the project structural/fixing designer must assess; please do not provide a generic anchor or substrate capacity for an unspecified building.
6. **Handover and maintenance:** Provide applicable installation, inspection, servicing and replacement instructions, available CAD/BIM references, warranty conditions and the route for technical support. Confirm which instructions supersede earlier documents.
