## Project planner and visualiser

The planner accepts approximate measurements and as many as three JPEG, PNG or PDF files totalling 3 MB. It keeps window height, floor-to-ceiling height and proposed finished drop distinct. A professional preselection and trade preselection reuse the same endpoint and triage email. Photos are decoded and resized to JPEG without the original metadata. PDF checks establish basic format and reject common active markers. PDFs remain untrusted attachments in the mail workflow. The endpoint returns a delivery error when the provider rejects the message. Product capability is determined after review and survey.

The weight estimator models the textile mass of one rectangular curtain layer, including face fabric, lining and retained make-up allowance. It illustrates how area and fullness scale mass. Separate layers must be assessed separately. The elevation demonstrates centre and one-way travel and independent curtain and voile control at two proportions. Both tools describe what their outputs cover and where survey and specification are required.

The current Apex visualiser uses authored room scenes, masks and lighting maps for fixed, tied-back shaped curtains. A photorealistic open/closed scene needs matched source photographs or rendered plates, separate geometry and masks for each layer and state, validated perspective and an accepted mobile size budget. The archived CurtainsUK work references a separate product and Shopify workflow. It provides no approved, drop-in tall-scene asset set in the Apex checkout. The elevation provides reliable movement planning now. Extend the photorealistic visualiser when a suitable licensed or approved matched asset set and defined model enter the project.
