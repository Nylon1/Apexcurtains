# Atelier brochure imagery

The brochure keeps the three general curtain examples together in the heading guide: pencil pleat, double pinch pleat and wave. All other editorial imagery is fabric, colour or making. Customer photographs remain linked privately to their own window and fabric records.

- `fabric-swatch-still-life.webp`: original editorial image created with the built-in image-generation tool on 24 October 2026. General fabric inspiration, never described as a customer's chosen fabric or a named supplier sample. Prompt: an overhead editorial photograph of overlapping, pinked-edge curtain fabric swatches in ivory linen, oatmeal herringbone, taupe, sage, mushroom velvet and navy velvet on warm stone, with soft daylight and small brass scissors. No text or brand marks.
- `apex-voucher.webp`: original voucher image created with the built-in image-generation tool on 24 October 2026. Prompt: a luxurious, straight overhead photograph of a midnight-navy invitation card on ivory linen with an oatmeal fabric swatch; antique-gold double hairline border and serif lettering. Exact text: "APEX CURTAINS", "WITH OUR COMPLIMENTS", "10% OFF", "ANY FUTURE ORDER". No other text, dates, codes, terms, barcode or QR code. The customer's unique code is rendered as live text below the image.
- Both original PNGs are preserved in the task's generated-images folder; the project copies are compressed WebP assets.

Existing website imagery is reused directly: `/headings/pencil-pleat.jpeg`, `/headings/pinch-pleat.jpg`, `/headings/wave-pleat.jpg`, `/journey/make-beautifully.jpeg`, `/guides/colour-wheel.svg`, and `/fabrics/oslo/linen.jpg`. The latter is labelled Oslo Linen by Prestigious Textiles and clearly identified as an example, not the customer's selection. Website image captions and links are shared by the PDF and HTML preview in `lib/atelier/brochure.ts`.
